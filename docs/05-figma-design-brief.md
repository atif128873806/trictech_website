# Figma UX/UI design brief

**Status:** Design input baseline v1.0 · 2026-09-15  
**Start condition:** Met — public website scope, PHP/Laravel stack, and current-site content approved by the project owner.

## Design objective

Design a new public experience that helps prospective customers understand Tricetech Solutions' services and request a relevant technical conversation. Use the PRD and SRS as the design inputs, and the source inventory only for approved business facts.

## Required Figma deliverables

1. Sitemap and primary user journeys.
2. Low-fidelity wireframes for company overview, both service families, and inquiry/validation/error/success states.
3. At least one visual direction developed for this project, with design rationale.
4. Responsive designs for agreed mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.
5. Reusable components and documented interaction states: navigation, links/buttons, service modules, form controls, errors, success, focus.
6. Clickable prototype for service discovery and inquiry journeys.
7. Handoff notes identifying content, interactions, breakpoints, and assets to implement.

The final number of pages and components follows the approved information architecture, not the current site's page count.

## Exploration prompts

- How can an IT leader quickly find the service most relevant to an existing technical problem?
- How can a nontechnical decision maker understand the value without losing technical precision?
- How should proof, company facts, and contact routes be arranged to support confidence?
- What is the minimum useful inquiry information, and how can error recovery stay calm and clear?

## Constraints

- Do not copy the current site's page layout, color palette, typography, imagery, card style, or decorative patterns.
- Use the approved site facts and service meanings; write fresh copy for the new information architecture.
- Design keyboard focus, input labels, validation, success/error feedback, and responsive behavior as first-class states.
- Do not imply features the site does not publish, such as a client portal or instant support.
- Approved current-site claims and technology names may be used in their stated context. Do not add vendor logos, testimonials, case studies, certifications, or new numerical claims without a source and permission.
- Ensure essential pages and inquiry controls can be implemented as Laravel server-rendered pages and remain usable without JavaScript.

## Design review criteria

- A reviewer can map each screen and flow to a PRD goal and SRS requirement.
- The prototype makes company identity, service fit, and contact route easy to find.
- All visible claims trace to the approved site or a later recorded approval.
- Mobile and keyboard journeys are complete.
- The design system is original to this project and documented well enough for implementation.

## Handoff gate

The business owner approves content structure and visual direction; the implementation owner reviews feasibility; unresolved behavior is recorded before coding. Figma is the design source of truth once approved.
